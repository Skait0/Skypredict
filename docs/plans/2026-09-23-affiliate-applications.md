### Description of the site (paste into "tell us about your website")

Soccerwizard is a free football prediction site for Nigerian bettors. A
statistical model rates 68 leagues and national teams every morning from
results data, and the site publishes a daily free booking code on SportyBet,
Bet9ja, BetKing and betPawa, plus a slip builder and a booking-code converter
that moves a slip from one bookmaker to another.

Every tip is graded after the match and the record is public, misses
included. That is the difference from tipster channels, and it is why readers
trust the codes.

- Our X account and Telegram channel, where the daily code is posted.
- No paid search on your brand, no incentivised traffic, no spam.
