Questions

What is a booking code?

A short code that loads a whole slip at the bookmaker in one go. We build the slip from our model's picks; you open the code on SportyBet, Bet9ja, BetKing, betPawa, 1xBet or Betway and decide whether to stake.

Does it renew?

No. Every pass is paid once through Paystack and simply ends. Nothing is saved to charge you again.
