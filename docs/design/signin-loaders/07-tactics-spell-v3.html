The move, beat by beat

  1. Summon: the board swoops up in 3D, pitch lines draw themselves, and a rune ring spins in the centre circle.
  2. Beam-in: players drop onto the pitch through pillars of gold light. Chalk defenders appear.
  3. Build-up: passes zip across with a comet tail and a crackle of lightning. Every touch pulses and throws a rune.
  4. Rainbow flick: the ball lifts off the pitch over a defender (its shadow stays on the grass), and the defender shatters into gold shards.
  5. Bullet time: "CURLER…" and time slows to a crawl as the shot bends in.
  6. GOAL: the net bulges, a shockwave rolls across the board, the sheet flashes gold, stars burst, and the board slams flat as "You're in, Tunde" lands. Android phones buzz.

It shows the loading steps in order: signing in during the summon, pulling your slips during the build-up, then ready at the goal. On a fast connection it plays about 2× faster, never cut short.
