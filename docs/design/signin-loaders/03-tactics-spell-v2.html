What's new

  • Board tilts in 3D, like a tactics table seen from the dugout.
  • Spark is a real ball with a gold comet tail.
  • Every touch casts: a ring pulses and a rune floats up, e.g. "1-2", "✦", "NUTMEG".
  • Defenders vanish: chalk X's spin into sparks as the move goes past them.
  • Pass counter in the corner ties to the real loading steps.
  • Goal! The shot curls in, the net bulges, the board shakes, gold stars burst, and "You're in, Tunde ✦" pops up. Android phones give a tiny buzz, if allowed.

When loading is fast

If your account loads before the move ends, the rest speeds up rather than being skipped, so the goal always lands in about a second.
